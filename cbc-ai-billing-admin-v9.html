5 easy steps to pay

  1. Tap Pay on your preferred plan.
  2. Select your payment method (MTN or Airtel), fill in your details and tap Pay.
  3. Enter your MoMo PIN on your phone and tap Send.
  4. After confirming, tap the blue Continue button.
  5. Critical: open the Receipts page, fill in your details and tap Verify & Activate.
